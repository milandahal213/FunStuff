FunStuff

Small interactive sites to play with.

Fractal Atlas

Zoom, tune and redraw 21 classic fractals, including a Mandelbrot deep zoom to about 10³⁰× that you can glide into.

 Beyond the Rubber Sheet

Seven 3D views of curved spacetime from general relativity: why things fall, black hole lensing, slowed clocks, orbits, light cones, tidal stretching and gravitational waves.

 Inside a Neural Network

Seven chapters from one neuron to a network you can train: weights, decision lines, learning by gradient descent, hidden layers, curve fitting, a full playground and image filters.

 Picturing the Mahabharata

The epic’s geography, timescale and cast: a living map of the journeys, lifelines down to the eighteen days of war, a scroll-through story, and a family tree of everyone in it.
