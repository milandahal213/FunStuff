Through the Looking Glass

Four small experiments that mix 3D with the real world, straight from the browser — no app to install. Point your phone at the room, or use a laptop webcam. A Google Cardboard viewer unlocks the VR and 3D-photo modes.

📦 Place in your space

Drop a 3D model (yours or a sample) onto the floor in front of you and walk around it, at real scale.

 ✏️ Draw in the air

Tap real surfaces to stick down cubes, spheres and cones in colour. Falls back to a camera window where AR isn't supported.

 🥽 Cardboard VR

A split-screen stereo scene you steer by turning your head. Works with any Cardboard-style viewer; uses native VR where available.

 📷 3D photo booth

Shoot a left-eye and right-eye frame, then export a red/cyan anaglyph, a Cardboard side-by-side pair, or a wiggle GIF.

What runs where. "Place in your space" works on Android and iPhone. "Draw in the air" gives true world-anchored AR on Android (WebXR); on iPhone and laptops it falls back to a steer-able camera window. "Cardboard VR" and the "3D photo booth" work on all three. Everything needs HTTPS and camera permission — fine on GitHub Pages. On iPhone the browser engine is always Safari/WebKit, so WebXR isn't available there regardless of which browser you use.
